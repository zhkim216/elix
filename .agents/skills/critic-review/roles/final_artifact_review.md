# Final Artifact Review Checklist

Use this checklist for saved reports, figures, wiki entries, and final
user-facing deliverables. This is not a replacement for data-pipeline review of
intermediate generated datasets.

## Checks

- The artifact answers the user's requested question or deliverable directly.
- Row, field, figure, and section semantics are clear enough to avoid
  misinterpretation.
- The artifact includes the necessary provenance, inputs, commands, assumptions,
  and reproducibility notes for its purpose.
- Summaries do not hide important exclusions, failed checks, missing data, or
  residual risks.
- Figures or tables are legible, labeled, and consistent with the underlying
  data.
- Final artifacts are stored in the requested location and format, with no
  misleading stale or duplicate outputs.
