# Grounded Answer Review Checklist

Use this checklist for factual answers, local evidence claims, citations, and
uncertainty handling.

## Checks

- Claims are supported by inspected local files, commands, artifacts, or cited
  external sources.
- File paths, row counts, field meanings, dates, and command outputs are
  represented accurately.
- The answer distinguishes raw source rows, intermediate caches, and derived
  evidence when that matters.
- Direct quotes and paraphrases do not overstate source content.
- Uncertainty, temporal drift, missing evidence, and skipped verification are
  stated plainly.
- Recommendations or conclusions do not exceed the inspected evidence.
